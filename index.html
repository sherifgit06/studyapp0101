Quick answers

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no tiers, no upgrade nags. You sign up so the app remembers who you are; eventually a backend will sync across devices, but the core will stay free.

Where does my data live?

In your browser's localStorage. Nothing leaves your machine. You can export to JSON for backups and import on another browser.

Does it work offline?

Once the page is loaded, yes — the entire app runs on the client. No network needed.

What about Step 2, OSCEs, MBBS exams?

The exam countdown is generic — name and date. Add as many as you like. Subjects can be customized too.

Can I track Anki time even if I review elsewhere?

Yes — start a focus block, set the resource to "Anki", and at the end log how many cards you reviewed. Running totals appear in your stats.

Is the login secure?

Right now the auth is local-only — passwords are hashed with SHA-256 and stored in your browser. It exists so the app feels real and so a future backend can drop in cleanly. Don't reuse a password you care about.
